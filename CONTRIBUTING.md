# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve the project's narrow safety boundary.

Before proposing a change:

1. Do not add member, banking, email, credential, or private archival data.
2. Do not add a provider-specific model SDK as a runtime dependency without discussion.
3. Add a test that demonstrates both the intended behavior and the relevant failure case.
4. Keep examples synthetic, public-domain, or explicitly licensed.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the MIT License.
